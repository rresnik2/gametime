Accessibility & motion (applies everywhere)

From the product plan's Accessibility line and PHASE_1_PLAN §4B.

  1. Touch targets ≥ 44 pt (minTouchTarget). Buttons and pills are drawn at 44 pt. Game chips (30 pt) and text links get a hit area that extends to 44 pt.
  2. Screen readers. Every rail and icon button has a label that includes its count ("Send a heart, 3.4 thousand"). The feed card is one element announcing "nova, live, Kart Racing, 1.2 thousand watching, Ranked grind to diamond", with custom actions Next stream / Previous stream / Send heart / Open chat, because swipe and double-tap gestures are taken by the screen reader.
  3. Reduced motion (system setting, or Settings → Reduce motion): no LIVE pulse, no Go Live ring pulse, no floating hearts, no skeleton shimmer, no countdowns, no bouncing hint arrow. The feed still snaps (it's navigation) but without overshoot. Chat messages still fade, just without sliding.
  4. Contrast. Text over video always sits on the bottom scrim or a 45–60% dark chip so it meets WCAG AA on any stream. Muted text #A1A1B5 on #0B0B12 passes AA; don't use it on surface at sizes under 12 pt.
  5. Haptics: one tick on swipe snap, follow and heart; never repeated. Respect the system haptics setting.
  6. Keyboard (web): ↑/↓ next and previous, M mute, C focus chat, Esc closes sheets. Visible focus ring in primary violet.
  7. Captions are v2 (auto-captions); the slot exists in the ⋯ menu.
  8. Text size: layouts must survive the largest Dynamic Type / font scale; stream titles truncate, chat wraps.

Open product questions

Raised while drawing these. Each needs an answer (or "go with the drawn default") at the design review; decisions go in docs/decisions/.

  1. Landscape streams in a vertical feed. Answered by decision 011 (Nov 16, 2026): every stream plays in its game's orientation, so landscape streams rotate to landscape full screen on phones; nothing is cropped. The fit-to-width drawing stays as the fallback when "Rotate to fit each stream" is off. Landscape variants of the feed card, overlay and Go Live picker still need drawing. Follow-ups: auto-rotate on by default? Swipe stays vertical in landscape? Tablets adapt the frame only?
  2. Onboarding: skip, and how many games? Drawn: 3 minimum, no skip. Should Edit profile also enforce 3?
  3. Cold start. Drawn: "Usually live" streamers + Go Live CTA, an end card instead of looping, and an optional hand-set team schedule card. Do we want the schedule card (needs a small config table)? Loop or end card?
  4. Under-13 retries. Remember a failed age check on the device?
  5. Go Live on phones. Streaming needs OBS on a computer. Drawn: phones can set up, see the key and run the dashboard, with a banner pointing to the web. Or should phones only show "Go to your computer" plus the dashboard?
  6. Chat filters scope. Drawn: built-in list + hide links + your own words, and a "followers only" proposal. Needs a migration (no table in schema v1) and server-side enforcement. What does the sender see?
  7. Preview before going public? Drawn: the stream goes public the moment OBS connects (webhook → live). A "preview, then press Go live" step would need the app to set live, which clients can't do today.
  8. Summary stats. Peak viewers and unique viewers aren't stored in schema v1. Add them, or drop them from the summary?
  9. Profile content. Clips are v2 and the MVP doesn't record video. Drawn: "Recent streams" (title, game, date, length). OK?
  10. Report reasons and auto-block. Agree the reason list. Should "Also block" be ticked by default?
  11. Who reviews reports during the class, and where (Supabase dashboard)?
  12. v2 placeholders. Keep a disabled Captions item and the Squads tab, or hide v2 features entirely?
  13. Share links. Drawn: web URLs /s/[streamId] and /u/[username], opening without onboarding. Needs routes and app links.
  14. Pop-out chat for streamers on desktop: MVP or later?
  15. Tablet portrait. The plan says chat docks right on tablet; drawn: chat under the video in portrait, right-docked in landscape. OK?
  16. Anonymous viewing (D4, decision 004). These frames assume signed-out viewers can watch but must sign up to chat, follow, heart or report, and that onboarding picks survive sign-up. D4 decides how.
